# Conventions

- `official/` holds the PDFs exactly as downloaded (not in git); `official/manifest.json` (in git) pins URL, version stamp, size and SHA-256 of each.
- `data/questions.*`, `data/images/`, `questions/*.md` and `results/crosscheck_en.json` are generated by `scripts/build_dataset.py` and never edited by hand.
- Question `id` = `"<group>-<number>"` as printed in the PDF (`"4-145"` = group 4, question 145). Numbering restarts at 1 in every group.
- `answer_index` is 0-based in JSON; the CSV column `answer` is 1-based, like the PDF's "Պատ.՝ N".
- Question text is kept exactly as published. The source mixes `եւ` and `և` (323 vs 194 in the questions); normalize only inside an analysis, never in the data.
- Every fact in README.md about fees, dates, exam rules or the law carries the date it was checked and its source.
- Old-pool ids are `"old-<group>-<k>"`: the k-th card of the group in the 2022 PDF (those PDFs have no question numbers). `data/old_pool/` and `results/pool_diff.json` are generated by `scripts/build_old_pool.py`.
- A shortcut claim needs a count from code: fires, right, and what guessing gets (sum of 1/k). Rules found by reading are marked as such; held-out and out-of-sample (old questions removed in 2026) numbers are reported next to in-sample ones.
