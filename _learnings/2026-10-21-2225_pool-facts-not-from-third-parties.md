# The official pool is 1,050 questions, not the 1,116 quoted elsewhere

repatarmenia.org and other pages say "1116 questions in total". The official A/B/C PDFs, regenerated
2026-10-02 (`?v=1790956366`; Armenian group 4 again 2026-10-05, `?v=1791194701`), contain 1,050:
145, 82, 59, 179, 136, 95, 129, 55, 119, 51 per group, each equal to the count printed on the
group's title band. Take pool facts from the PDFs, not from third-party pages or apps.

Other facts measured on the pool (`data/questions.json`):

- 2-5 options per question: 287 / 568 / 146 / 49 questions with 2 / 3 / 4 / 5.
- 715 questions have a picture (699 distinct pictures; 15 pictures are reused by different questions,
  never with a contradicting answer).
- 76 question texts are used more than once, covering 273 questions (26%): same wording, the picture
  decides the answer. 9 pairs even have identical options; different pictures, sometimes different
  answers (1-111 vs 1-118).
