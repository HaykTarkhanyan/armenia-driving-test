# The 2022 question PDFs (Word exports) have quirks a parser must expect

Found while parsing the pool that was online until autumn 2026 (`scripts/build_old_pool.py`):

- **No question numbers, no colours.** Two-column tables of cards; the answer line closes a card.
- **The answer line uses U+2024 (ONE DOT LEADER), not a full stop:** `Պատ․՝3`
  (`Պ:054A ա:0561 տ:057F ․:2024 ՝:055D`, all 147 lines of group 1). Once the dot is missing
  entirely: `Պատ ՝3` (old group 4, card 7). The parser failed loudly on it ("2 pictures" in one card,
  because the card never closed and swallowed the next one) instead of producing a wrong question.
- **Question text only inside the picture** for two cards (old 4-95: "Ո՞ր նշանն է արագության
  սահմանափակում մտցնում"). Matched to the new pool by options + picture hash.
- **Picture labels are Armenian letters** (Ա/Բ/Գ) where the 2026 PDFs use Latin (A/B/C): "Միայն Բ"
  vs "Միայն B" made identical options look changed until the matcher mapped them.
- **The old English PDFs are a different edition:** group 1 has 132 answer lines in English vs 147 in
  Armenian (22 vs 25 pages), so they cannot be compared card by card.
- Pictures were redrawn for 2026 (old 600x300 cartoons, new 1200x600 renders): byte hashes never
  match, perceptual hashes do (distance 0-10 for the same scene, up to 32 when redrawn).
